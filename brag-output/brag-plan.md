# Brag Plan — WA Bot Hub

## What is it
Premium multi-instance WhatsApp automation platform. One server, unlimited connected numbers, managed from a sleek dashboard or Telegram.

## Who it's for
Anyone running WhatsApp at scale — marketing ops, support teams, multi-account businesses.

## What sets it apart
- Unlimited sessions per bot — not 1 per server
- Auto-rotate to backup numbers when one logs out — zero downtime
- Manage everything from Telegram: add sessions, upload APKs, distribute coupons
- AI-powered auto-replies
- Play Store–style public download pages per instance

## Tone
`default` — punchy, playful, clean

## Visual Identity
- Background: #06050b (deep dark)
- Primary: #7c3aed (neon purple)
- Secondary: #06b6d4 (neon cyan)
- WhatsApp green: #22c55e
- Font: Plus Jakarta Sans (headings), JetBrains Mono (code)
- Ambient blobs (purple, cyan) blurred in background
- Glass-morphism cards with subtle borders

## Visual Hook
The dashboard's ambient glow + session grid. Real UI from the project.

## Scene-by-scene storyboard (target: 20s, 30fps)

### Scene 1 — Hook (2s)
Dark screen (#06050b). Two glowing blobs (purple + cyan) pulse gently. Text fades in: "One server."

### Scene 2 — Reveal (3s)
The WA Bot Hub dashboard slides in — real project UI. Sidebar with icons (Dashboard, Auto-Responders, Sessions, APK Store). Green phone icons in the session grid. Title: "WA Bot Hub" in white. Subtitle: "Unlimited numbers. Zero downtime."

### Scene 3 — Highlight 1: Auto-rotation (5s)
Split view: left shows a phone turning red (logged out), right shows green phones taking over. Amber arrow animates between them. The session grid pulses green. Text: "Auto-rotates when numbers go offline."

### Scene 4 — Highlight 2: Telegram control (5s)
Phone screen mockup with Telegram interface: chat bubbles appear sequentially — "/addsession", "📱 Adding session...", "🎁 BLINK50 → delivered". Typing dots pulse. Text: "Manage everything from Telegram."

### Scene 5 — Highlight 3: AI + Coupons (3s)
Quick flash of AI reply in chat bubble, then coupon card with progress bar filling up and "Delivered" checkmark. Text: "AI replies. Smart coupon delivery."

### Scene 6 — Punchline (2s)
Dashboard hero settles. Text: "Build it. Ship it. Let it run." CTA button: "github.com/Zazbhai/whatsapp-bot"

Total: 20 seconds
